## 🎯 What is Ninja Subtitler Pro?

**Ninja Subtitler Pro** is a powerful desktop application that automatically generates **word-level accurate subtitles** for your videos using AI. Perfect for:

- 📱 **Social Media Creators** – TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- 🎬 **YouTubers** – Add professional captions to your videos
- 📚 **Educators** – Make your content accessible
- 🌍 **Multi-language Creators** – Support for 70+ languages with transliteration

> **No cloud uploads. No subscriptions. Your videos stay on YOUR computer.**

## 🎥 How It Works

```
┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐
│   Load Video    │────▶│  AI Transcribe  │────▶│ Style Subtitles │
│   (MP4, MOV)    │     │  (Groq/Whisper) │     │ (15+ Styles)    │
└─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘
                                                         │
┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐             │
│  Export Video   │◀────│   Preview &     │◀────────────┘
│  (GPU Accel.)   │     │     Edit        │
└─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘
```

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Import** your video (drag & drop or browse)
2. **Transcribe** with Groq AI or local Whisper
3. **Choose** your highlight style and animation
4. **Customize** fonts, colors, and positioning
5. **Preview** your subtitles in real-time
6. **Export** with GPU-accelerated encoding

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Is my video uploaded to the cloud?</strong></summary>

No! Your video stays on your computer. Only the extracted audio is sent to Groq's API for transcription. You can also use Local Whisper for 100% offline processing.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I get a Groq API key?</strong></summary>

1. Visit [console.groq.com](https://console.groq.com)
2. Sign up for free
3. Go to API Keys
4. Create a new key
5. Copy and paste into Ninja Subtitler Pro
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why is export slow?</strong></summary>

If you have an NVIDIA or Intel GPU, the app will automatically use hardware acceleration. If you're using CPU encoding, it will be slower. GPU encoding is typically 5-10x faster.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I use this for TikTok/YouTube Shorts?</strong></summary>

Absolutely! This tool is perfect for short-form content. Just export your video and upload directly to your favorite platform.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does the free version add a watermark?</strong></summary>

No watermarks! The free version is fully functional, just with daily limits.
</details>
